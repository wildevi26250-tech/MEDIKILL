# MEDIKILL
## Controls
- Movement is WASD
- Jump is space bar
- to wall run, run at a wall on an angle
- SHIFT to wall run up
- CTRL to wall run down
- To get rid of tutorial text do what its telling you
- You can stop slow-motion by shooting (this will not get rid of the tex)
- Shoot is LMB
- Aim is RMB
- If you jump and aim you go into bullet time and do more damage
## Guns
### The list of guns included 
- Pistol 10 damage
- AR 20 damage
- Sniper 70 damage but you have to manually reload
## Fully Functioning Enemy AI
- They shoot
- There shooting patterns are randomized so they don't always hit
- They have patrol points that they walk around
- They have a chasing mode
- And a running away mode if there health gets to low (this may not work)
- This is what I'm most proud of and what took a lot of my time
